# NorthPlay 2027 Business Strategy
*Becoming Ontario's most trusted sportsbook*

Leadership Team | Confidential | v1.2 | October 14, 2026
> NorthPlay is a fictional operator. All figures are illustrative demo data.


## Slide 2: 2026 in review

- **212K**: active players (Oct 2026)
- **$41M**: net gaming revenue YTD
- **31%**: players with a limit set
- **$148**: avg. acquisition cost, up 38% YoY

- Live betting and the parlay builder now drive 54% of handle
- Acquisition costs rose sharply after Ontario's ban on public bonus advertising
- Two creative escalations from compliance in 2026, both tied to bonus language

## Slide 3: Market context

- Ontario is a mature, crowded regulated market with 45+ operators competing for the same players
- The regulator keeps tightening: athlete ban (Feb 2024), public inducement ban, closer scrutiny of 'free' offers
- Bonus-led acquisition is no longer a durable advantage; brand trust and product are
- Regulatory findings now carry real financial penalties and public reputational damage

## Slide 4: Our ambition

**Be the sportsbook Ontario adults trust most.**

- Mission: make following the game more fun, without putting players at risk
- North Star metric: 90-day retained players who have at least one NorthPlay Limits tool enabled
- We win on product and trust, not on bonuses

## Slide 5: Three strategic pillars

- **1. Trust-first brand**: Responsible gambling is our brand differentiator, not a disclaimer. Every touchpoint is factual, calm and compliant.
- **2. Product-led growth**: Invest in live betting, same-game parlays and micro-markets. Let features, not offers, sell the app.
- **3. Hockey-first**: Own NHL betting in Ontario. Hockey is ~40% of our handle and our strongest brand association.

## Slide 6: Who we serve

- Core: sports fans aged 25-54 in Ontario who already watch NHL, NFL and NBA
- We do not build campaigns aimed at 19-24 year olds; minimum targeting age is 25 on all paid channels
- We do not target lapsed players with re-activation offers
- Messaging assumes an experienced, entertainment-minded fan, not someone looking to make money

## Slide 7: Shifting the growth model

|  | 2026 | 2027 plan |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus-led acquisition | 45% of marketing budget | 10% (opted-in direct only) |
| Brand and product marketing | 35% | 60% |
| Retention and CRM | 20% | 30% |
| Target acquisition cost | $148 | $120 |

- All inducements move to on-site and consented direct channels only

## Slide 8: Responsible gambling as a differentiator

- Launch 'NorthPlay Limits', a single hub for deposit, time and loss limits (Q1 2027)
- Target: 40% of active players with a limit set by Dec 2027
- Retired CFL lineman Marcus Doyle stays on as Play Smart ambassador, for responsible gambling messaging only
- Publish an annual Player Protection Report

## Slide 10: Risks and guardrails

- Regulatory risk: any finding damages the trust-first strategy. Zero tolerance on compliance shortcuts
- All creative is reviewed by the Legal Approver Agent before trafficking; target turnaround under 24 hours
- Legal decisions made in #legal-marketing are binding and take effect immediately, even before decks are updated
- Partner and ambassador use requires a current, signed agreement
