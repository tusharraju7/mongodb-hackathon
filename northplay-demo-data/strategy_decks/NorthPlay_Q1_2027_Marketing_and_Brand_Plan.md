## Slide 5: Brand voice

- **We are**: Confident, factual, calm, fan-first. We talk about the game, the odds and the product.
- **We never**: Use hype or certainty words ('lock', 'easy money', 'guaranteed'), slang, urgency ('last chance'), or talk about money outcomes.
- **We always**: Show odds as information, include the full disclaimer, and assume an experienced adult fan.

## Slide 6: Visual identity

- Palette: Deep Navy #06141F, Rink Green #145C4A, Mint #7FE0C4. No neon, pink or orange gradients
- Typography: Inter / Inter Display
- No cartoon characters or mascots
- No people who look under 25. Hands, crowds and arenas are preferred over faces
- Logo: the NorthPlay arrow and wordmark, always top-left

## Slide 7: Messaging pillars

- **Every play, live**: Live odds and in-game betting for hockey fans
- **Built for the game**: Parlay builder, props, micro-markets
- **Play smart**: NorthPlay Limits, taking breaks, setting a budget

## Slide 8: Ambassadors and partnerships

- Marcus Doyle (retired CFL lineman): Play Smart ambassador, responsible gambling messaging only, no odds or betting CTAs in his creative
- No other athlete ambassadors in Q1
- Team and league marks may only be used under a signed license. We hold no NHL or NFL licenses
- Influencer and creator partnerships are not planned for Q1

## Slide 9: Mandatory creative elements

- Full disclaimer on every asset: '19+. Ontario only. Please play responsibly. Concerned about your gambling? Contact ConnexOntario 1-866-531-2600.'
- Minimum disclaimer size: 24px on 1080x1080 and 1080x1920; 18px on 1200x628
- Audio: the disclaimer is read at normal pace, never sped up
- Offers appear only in opted-in email/SMS and on-site, with full terms at first presentation

## Slide 10: Approval workflow

- 1. Creative team uploads the asset to the Legal Approver Agent
- 2. Agent returns approve / approve with changes / reject, with cited rules and precedents
- 3. Rejections are escalated to Legal (#legal-marketing) only if the team disputes them
- 4. Nothing is trafficked without an approval ID
- Turnaround target: under 24 hours
