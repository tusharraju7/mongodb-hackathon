# REG-11: No Linking Gambling to Status, Attractiveness or Peer Pressure

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Fictional rules modelled on Ontario-style internet gaming advertising standards, written for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not any regulator's official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-11
- **Category:** Truthful Advertising
- **Source:** NorthPlay Advertising Policy (modelled on Ontario-style iGaming advertising standards), Section 2.04, Req. 3, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14
- **Severity:** major
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not suggest gambling enhances self-image, self-esteem, control, status, recognition or admiration; must not suggest peer pressure or disparage abstaining; must not link gambling to seduction, sexual success or attractiveness; must not portray gambling as a sign of toughness or a rite of passage; and must not use well-known personalities to suggest gambling contributed to their success.

## Intent
Gambling should not be framed as a path to social success.

## What the reviewer checks for
- "Be the legend of the group chat", "real fans bet"
- Mocking friends who don't bet
- Romantic or attractive partners drawn to the winner
- "Grown men bet" or coming-of-age framing (e.g., 'You're 19. Time to bet.')

## Non-compliant examples
- "Turned 19? Welcome to the big leagues. Place your first bet."
- "Still not betting? Your friends are."

## Compliant examples
- Neutral copy about features without social status claims

## Remediation
Remove status, attractiveness, peer pressure and rite-of-passage framing.
