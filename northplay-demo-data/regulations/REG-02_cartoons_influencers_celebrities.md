# REG-02: No Cartoons, Influencers or Celebrities Likely to Appeal to Minors

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-02
- **Category:** Protection of Minors
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.03, Req. 3
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not use cartoon figures, symbols, role models, social media influencers, celebrities or entertainers who would likely be expected to appeal to minors.

## Intent
Many personalities and animated characters have large under-19 audiences; using them reaches minors even if the ad is aimed at adults.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Animated mascots or cartoon characters
- Social media creators, streamers or YouTubers with young audiences
- Pop stars, actors or entertainers popular with teens
- Emoji-heavy or sticker-style characters as the focal point

## Non-compliant examples
- A cartoon hockey puck mascot cheering "Bet with Pucky!"
- Popular teen gaming streamer promoting the app

## Compliant examples
- Abstract brand graphics with no characters
- An adult presenter with no youth-oriented following

## Remediation
Remove the character or personality. If a mascot is used, it must not primarily appeal to minors; when in doubt, remove it.
