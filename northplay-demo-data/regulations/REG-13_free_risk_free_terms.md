# REG-13: 'Free' and 'Risk-Free' Claims and Terms Disclosure

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Fictional rules modelled on Ontario-style internet gaming advertising standards, written for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not any regulator's official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-13
- **Category:** Inducements
- **Source:** NorthPlay Advertising Policy (modelled on Ontario-style iGaming advertising standards), Section 2.06
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Where inducement advertising is permitted (on-site or consented direct marketing), it must disclose all material conditions and limitations at first presentation (with other conditions no more than one click away); must not describe an offer as 'free' if the player must risk their own money or conditions attach to their money; and must not describe a bet as 'risk-free' if the player must incur any loss or risk their own money to use it or withdraw winnings.

## Intent
Players must understand the true cost and conditions of any offer.

## What the reviewer checks for
- The words 'free', 'free bet', 'risk-free', 'no risk', 'on us'
- Offers requiring a deposit or wager to unlock
- Missing wagering requirements, expiry, min odds or max payout
- Terms hidden behind multiple clicks

## Non-compliant examples
- Email: "Your risk-free first bet is waiting!" (requires $50 deposit)
- On-site banner with bonus but no wagering terms shown

## Compliant examples
- "Bet $50, get $50 in bonus bets. 1x playthrough, min odds -200, expires in 7 days. Full terms."

## Remediation
Remove 'free'/'risk-free' unless literally true; show material terms at first presentation.
