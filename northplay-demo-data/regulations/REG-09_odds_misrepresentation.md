# REG-09: No Misrepresenting the Odds of Winning

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-09
- **Category:** Truthful Advertising
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.04, Req. 5 and Req. 6
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not make false promises or present winning as the probable outcome, must not imply that the chances of winning increase with time played or money spent, and must not suggest skill influences outcomes where it does not.

## Intent
Players must understand that losing is the most likely outcome of any bet.

## What the reviewer checks for
- "Guaranteed", "sure thing", "lock", "can't lose", "easy money"
- "Bet more, win more" or "the more you play, the better your chances"
- Win-rate claims or showcasing only winning outcomes as typical

## Non-compliant examples
- "Tonight's lock: Leafs ML. Easy money."
- "The more you bet, the higher your chance of winning."

## Compliant examples
- Displaying current odds factually (e.g., 'Leafs +120') with no outcome promises

## Remediation
Remove certainty language and any claims linking spend or time to winning.
