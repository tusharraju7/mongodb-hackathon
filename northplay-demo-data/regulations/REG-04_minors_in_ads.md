# REG-04: No Minors or People Who Appear to Be Minors

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-04
- **Category:** Protection of Minors
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.03, Req. 5
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not include individuals who are, or appear to be, minors to promote gaming.

## Intent
Depicting young people normalizes gambling for youth audiences.

## What the reviewer checks for
- People who look under 25 in a way that could read as under 19
- Children or teens in any scene, including background family scenes
- School uniforms, youth sports gear

## Non-compliant examples
- Family watching a game with a teenager celebrating a bet win
- Young-looking talent in a dorm room setting

## Compliant examples
- Clearly adult talent (internal guideline: 25+ in appearance)

## Remediation
Recast with clearly adult talent and remove any minors from the scene.
