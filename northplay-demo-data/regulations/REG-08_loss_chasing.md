# REG-08: No Encouraging Loss Chasing

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-08
- **Category:** Truthful Advertising
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.04, Req. 4
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not encourage play as a means of recovering past gaming or other financial losses.

## Intent
Loss chasing is a core marker of problem gambling.

## What the reviewer checks for
- "Win it back", "bounce back", "make up for last week", "redemption bet"
- Offers framed around previous losses (e.g., 'lost last night? try again')

## Non-compliant examples
- "Rough Sunday? Win it all back on Monday Night Football."

## Compliant examples
- Game-preview ads that do not reference player losses

## Remediation
Remove any reference to prior losses or recovering money.
