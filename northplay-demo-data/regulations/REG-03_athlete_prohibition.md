# REG-03: Athlete Prohibition (Active or Retired)

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Fictional rules modelled on Ontario-style internet gaming advertising standards, written for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not any regulator's official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-03
- **Category:** Protection of Minors
- **Source:** NorthPlay Advertising Policy (modelled on Ontario-style iGaming advertising standards), Section 2.03, Req. 4 (in force Feb 28, 2024)
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not use active or retired athletes who have a direct or indirect agreement or arrangement with an operator or gaming-related supplier, except for the exclusive purpose of advocating responsible gambling practices.

## Intent
Athletes have broad appeal, including to minors. Ontario removed them from gambling ads except for responsible gambling messaging.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Any current or former professional, Olympic or college athlete
- Language such as 'former pro hockey star', 'pro', 'champion', 'ambassador' tied to a sports figure
- Athletes in jerseys or equipment endorsing betting
- Exception: athlete appears ONLY to promote responsible gambling, with no betting promotion, odds or offers in the same creative

## Non-compliant examples
- "NorthPlay ambassador and former pro hockey star Jake Moreau says: get in the game!"
- Retired quarterback holding a phone showing the NorthPlay app

## Compliant examples
- Former athlete speaking only about setting deposit limits and taking breaks, with no betting call-to-action

## Remediation
Remove the athlete, or rebuild the creative as a pure responsible gambling message with no promotional content.
