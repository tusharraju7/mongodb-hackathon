# REG-12: No Public Advertising of Bonuses, Credits or Inducements

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-12
- **Category:** Inducements
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.05
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising that communicates gambling inducements, bonuses and credits is prohibited, except on the operator's own gaming site and through direct marketing to players who have actively consented. This applies to all public advertising, including targeted and algorithm-based ads; inducements themselves are not banned.

## Intent
Bonus-driven public advertising was found to drive harmful sign-ups and play.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Deposit matches, bonus bets, odds boosts, free bets, sign-up credits, cash back, promo codes
- Any dollar amount tied to an offer (e.g., 'Get $200 in bonus bets')
- Channel: social, display, TV, radio, outdoor, search (public = prohibited)
- Exception: on-site banners or consented email/SMS/push

## Non-compliant examples
- Instagram ad: "Sign up today and get $500 in bonus bets!"
- Billboard: "100% deposit match up to $1,000"

## Compliant examples
- Brand awareness ad with no offer
- Promotional email to opted-in players with full terms

## Remediation
Remove the offer from public creative; deliver offers only on-site or to consented players.
