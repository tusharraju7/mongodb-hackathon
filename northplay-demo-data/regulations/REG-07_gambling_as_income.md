# REG-07: No Gambling as Income, Investment or Solution to Problems

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Fictional rules modelled on Ontario-style internet gaming advertising standards, written for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not any regulator's official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-07
- **Category:** Truthful Advertising
- **Source:** NorthPlay Advertising Policy (modelled on Ontario-style iGaming advertising standards), Section 2.04, Req. 1 and Req. 2
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not imply that gaming is required to meet family or social obligations or solve personal problems, and must not promote gaming as an alternative to employment, a financial investment, or a way to achieve financial security.

## Intent
Gambling is entertainment, not a financial strategy.

## What the reviewer checks for
- "Side hustle", "extra income", "pay your rent", "quit your job"
- Investment language: ROI, portfolio, returns, grow your money
- Gambling framed as a way to pay bills, debts or family costs

## Non-compliant examples
- "Rent due? Your next parlay could cover it."
- "Treat your bets like a portfolio. Grow your money with NorthPlay."

## Compliant examples
- "Sports betting is entertainment. Set a budget and stick to it."

## Remediation
Remove all financial outcome framing; position the product as entertainment.
