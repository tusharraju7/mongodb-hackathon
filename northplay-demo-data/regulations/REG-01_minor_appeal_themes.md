# REG-01: No Themes or Language Appealing to Minors

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Fictional rules modelled on Ontario-style internet gaming advertising standards, written for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not any regulator's official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-01
- **Category:** Protection of Minors
- **Source:** NorthPlay Advertising Policy (modelled on Ontario-style iGaming advertising standards), Section 2.03, Req. 1
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not be built on themes, or use language, intended to appeal primarily to minors.

## Intent
Keep gambling marketing from attracting people under the legal gambling age (19 in Ontario).

## What the reviewer checks for
- Youth slang, meme formats or school-related themes
- Video-game, toy, candy or back-to-school imagery
- Bright, childish visual style aimed at kids rather than adults
- References to youth sports leagues, high school or university varsity games

## Non-compliant examples
- "No cap, this parlay is bussin" with meme template visuals
- Ad framed around back-to-school season with lockers and backpacks

## Compliant examples
- Adult-oriented creative focused on the product, odds or the game itself, with standard disclaimers

## Remediation
Rewrite copy in neutral adult language and remove youth-oriented imagery or themes.
