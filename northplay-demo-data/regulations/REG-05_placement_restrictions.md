# REG-05: Placement Restrictions: Youth Media and Locations

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-05
- **Category:** Protection of Minors
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.03, Req. 2 and Req. 6
- **Severity:** blocker
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Ads must not appear on billboards or outdoor displays directly adjacent to schools or other primarily youth-oriented locations, or in media, venues, websites or digital channels directed primarily at minors or where most of the audience is reasonably expected to be minors.

## Intent
Even compliant creative becomes non-compliant if placed where minors are the main audience.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Placement metadata: outdoor locations near schools, arenas hosting youth events
- Channels: kids' gaming platforms, youth-focused apps, teen-oriented creators
- Audience data showing majority under-19 audience

## Non-compliant examples
- Billboard across the street from a high school
- Pre-roll on a children's gaming channel

## Compliant examples
- Placements with verified adult-majority audiences (19+)

## Remediation
Move the placement and confirm audience age composition before trafficking.
