# REG-10: No Socially Irresponsible or Escapist Gambling

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-10
- **Category:** Truthful Advertising
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.04, Req. 7, 8 and 9
- **Severity:** major
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not portray, condone or encourage socially irresponsible gambling or behaviour that could cause financial, social or emotional harm; must not suggest gambling is an escape from personal or professional problems; and must not portray gambling as indispensable or more important than family, friends, work or education.

## Intent
Avoid normalizing harmful gambling patterns.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Betting at work, during family events, or instead of responsibilities
- Gambling shown as stress relief or escape
- Excessive drinking combined with betting
- All-night or nonstop betting imagery

## Non-compliant examples
- Person betting under the table at a wedding
- "Bad day at work? Escape with NorthPlay."

## Compliant examples
- Friends watching a game together at home, with betting shown as a small part of the experience

## Remediation
Remove scenes of gambling displacing responsibilities or used as an escape.
