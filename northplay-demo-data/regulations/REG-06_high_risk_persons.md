# REG-06: No Exploiting or Enticing High-Risk Persons

> Demo policy document for the NorthPlay legal approver agent (NorthPlay is a fictional operator). Paraphrased from Ontario's AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming for hackathon use only; not legal advice and not the official text.

- **Rule ID:** REG-06
- **Category:** Responsible Gambling
- **Source:** AGCO Registrar's Standards for Internet Gaming (Ontario), Standard 2.03, Req. 7 and Req. 8
- **Severity:** major
- **Jurisdiction:** Ontario, Canada

## Rule
Advertising must not exploit the susceptibilities, aspirations, credulity, inexperience or lack of knowledge of potentially high-risk persons, or otherwise extoll the virtues of gaming. It must not entice or attract potentially high-risk players, and operators must limit marketing to known high-risk players.

## Intent
Protect people most vulnerable to gambling harm.

## What the reviewer checks for
- Urgency and pressure language ("Don't miss out", "Bet now before it's too late")
- Messaging aimed at people new to betting that hides how it works
- Glorifying gambling as exciting, life-changing or essential
- Late-night 'one more bet' style messaging

## Non-compliant examples
- "Never bet before? Don't worry, everyone wins on their first try!"
- "Can't sleep? Neither can the odds. Bet now."

## Compliant examples
- Plain description of product features with responsible gambling message

## Remediation
Remove pressure, urgency and glorifying language; describe the product factually.
